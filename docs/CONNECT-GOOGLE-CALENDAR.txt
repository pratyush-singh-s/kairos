HOW TO CONNECT GOOGLE CALENDAR TO KAIROS
========================================

You need a free Google "client ID" of your own (Part A, once, about 10 minutes).
Then you sign in with it (Part B). Nothing is billed. Google sometimes renames
buttons; if a name is slightly different, pick the closest match.

Already did Part A before? Skip to Part B.


PART A - MAKE YOUR CLIENT ID (ONCE)
-----------------------------------

1. Make a project
   - Open https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Gmail.
   - Agree to the terms if asked.
   - Top left: project picker (may say "Select a project") > New project.
   - Name: Kairos. Press Create, then select the Kairos project.

2. Turn on the Calendar API
   - In the top search bar type: Google Calendar API. Open it, press Enable.

3. Set up the sign-in screen
   - Search: Google Auth Platform (older name: OAuth consent screen). Press Get started.
   - App name: Kairos. Support email: your Gmail. Next.
   - Audience: External. Next.
   - Contact email: your Gmail. Next. Tick the agreement, Continue, Create.
   - Left menu > Audience > Test users > Add users > type your Gmail > Save.
     (Without this, Google says "Access blocked".)

4. Make the client
   - Left menu > Clients (older: Credentials > Create credentials > OAuth client ID).
   - Create client. Application type: Desktop app (NOT Web application).
     Name: Kairos desktop. Create.
   - Copy the Client ID (ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com) and the
     Client secret (starts with GOCSPX-). Press Download JSON too and keep it private.
   - Second PC: use the same client ID. If you no longer have the secret, open the
     client and press "Add secret" to get a new one.


PART B - SIGN IN (ON EACH PC)
-----------------------------

1. In Kairos open Settings > Data & accounts > Google account.
2. Paste the Client ID and Client secret. Press Save.
3. Press "Sign in with Google" and sign in on Google's page.
4. Tick "Sync Google Calendar automatically". Google asks again, for Calendar.
5. If Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app": press Continue
   (or Advanced > Go to Kairos). It is your own app, so this is expected.
6. Tick the calendar permission box, then Continue / Allow.
7. Close the browser tab and go back to Kairos.

Your Google Calendar now shows in Kairos and syncs every 5 minutes. Kairos also makes
a calendar called "Focus" in your Google account with your dated to-dos and deadlines.
Turn either off in Settings > Google account.


EVERY 7 DAYS: RECONNECT
-----------------------
If your Google app stays in "Testing" mode (Publish app on the Audience page avoids this), Google ends Calendar access after 7 days.
When Kairos shows a Reconnect bar, press it and allow again. Your events stay visible
meanwhile.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Access blocked" on Google's page: add your Gmail as a test user (Part A, step 3).
  Also check the Calendar API is enabled (step 2).
- "Google did not accept the client ID or secret": copy both again, no spaces.
- "Google rejected the return address": the client must be "Desktop app". Make a new one.
- Google page stuck or closed: press Connect again (or Cancel on the Calendar page).
- "Could not reach Google": check the internet, then Connect again.
- To remove access: Settings > Google account > Sign out, or
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
