# Focus 0.21.1: text colour in notes

- Notes have a **text colour** button (the "A" with a coloured bar, next to the size menu). Highlight some text, press it,
  and pick one of eight colours, **A** for the normal colour, or **+** to choose any colour. The bar under the "A" shows
  the colour where the cursor is.
- Colours sync to your other devices with the rest of the note.

Install: unzip **Focus-0.21.1-installer.zip** and run the setup. It upgrades in place and keeps your data. Install
the same version on your other PC so the two keep syncing. Focus Blocker stays at 0.13.0.
