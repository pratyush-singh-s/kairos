# Focus 0.21.2: three menu styles

- **Settings → Appearance → Menu** now has three choices:
  - **Full**: icons and names, as before 0.21.0.
  - **Icons only**: a narrow strip of icons. Hover an icon to see its name.
  - **Hidden**: pages fill the window. Move the mouse to the left edge, or swipe from the left, to bring the menu out.
- The menu button at the bottom of the menu, and **Ctrl+B**, switch to the next style (Full → Icons only → Hidden).
- If you hid the menu in 0.21.0 or 0.21.1, it starts as Hidden; otherwise it starts as Full.

Install: unzip **Focus-0.21.2-installer.zip** and run the setup. It upgrades in place and keeps your data. Install
the same version on your other PC so the two keep syncing. Focus Blocker stays at 0.13.0.
