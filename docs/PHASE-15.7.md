# Focus 0.15.0

Install `Focus-0.15.0-installer.zip` over your current Focus. Your tasks, deadlines and settings are kept.
The Firefox extension is unchanged (0.10.0).

## To-do spaces

- The To-do page now has tabs next to its title. **Everyday** is the board you had before; everything already on it stays there.
- **+ New space** makes another space. Pick one of two kinds:
  - **Week board**: days of the week, like Everyday, for another part of your life (e.g. College).
  - **Day-by-day plan**: Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 … with steps on each (e.g. Day 1 write the code, Day 2 debugging, Day 3 new version and QoL features).
- In a plan, set **Day 1 is** to a date and the days get real dates; today's day is outlined. Steps for today (and unticked ones from earlier days) also show on the dashboard.
- Drag steps between days, or into **No day yet** for ideas. **+ Add day** adds a day at the end. **Edit space** renames, recolours, changes the number of days, or deletes the space (with Undo).

## Deadlines

- The day grid can no longer be ticked. It fills in by itself as the days go by, so it only shows how much time is left.

## Settings

- **Custom** colours: in Settings → Appearance, the last swatch is Custom. Pick a background, text and accent colour; cards, borders and grey text are worked out from them. Light backgrounds work too. Glass and Terminal each keep their own custom colours.
- The palette you pick is now saved properly (0.14.0 only remembered it on screen).
- The description lines under Glass and Terminal are gone.

## Scrollbar

- Scrollbars are thin and stay hidden until your cursor is on them (near the right edge of the page).
