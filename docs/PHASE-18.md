# Focus 0.16.0: Notes (Phase 18)

Install `Focus-0.16.0-installer.zip` over your current Focus. Everything else is kept.

## Notes page

- **New note** makes a note and puts the cursor in its title. Press Enter in the title to start writing.
- Notes save by themselves a moment after you stop typing ("Saved" next to the title).
- The list on the left has pinned notes first, then the most recently edited. **Search notes** finds notes containing every word you type, even half-typed words ("phys" finds "physics").
- The pin button keeps a note at the top of the list and on the dashboard. The × deletes it (Undo for a few seconds).

## Formatting

| What | Button | Keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bold | B | Ctrl+B |
| Italic | I | Ctrl+I |
| Text size | Small / Normal / Large / Huge | |
| List | ⋮ List / 123 List | type `- ` or `1. ` at the start of a line |
| Superscript | x² | Ctrl+. or type `x^2` then a space |
| Subscript | x₂ | Ctrl+, or type `x_2` then a space |

**The List button (0.16.1):** it shows the list it will make. Press it once for a bullet list (⋮); it then shows 123, and pressing again turns it into a numbered list (and back). Highlight some lines first and only those lines become the list; the text around them stays as it was. To end a list, press Enter on an empty item.

Typing `_` followed by numbers (or one letter) and a space lowers them: `H_2 ` becomes H₂.

Typing `^` followed by letters or numbers and then a space (or . , ; : ! ?) raises them: `a^2 + b^2` becomes a² + b².

## Dashboard

Pinned notes show on the dashboard; click one to open it.

Notes stay on this PC for now. Syncing them to your other PC comes with the sync phase.
