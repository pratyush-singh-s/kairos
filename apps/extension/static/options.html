Kairos Blocker

Your sites, YouTube choices and theme are set in the Kairos app, on its Blocking page (theme in Settings). Change them there and every browser follows. YouTube tag groups can be changed here too. If Kairos is closed, the last lists keep working. Your sites, YouTube choices and theme come from your linked phone (they are the Kairos app's lists from your laptop). Change them in the Kairos app, or unlink the phone to edit them here.

      when blocking is on

      Blocking turns on during focus and off on breaks. It follows, in this order: the Kairos app on this PC, the Kairos phone app if you link it below, and the extension's own focus timer in the toolbar popup (set focus and break lengths and rounds, then Start).

      link your phone

      No Kairos app on this PC? Blocking can follow focus on the Kairos app on your Android phone instead. The phone and this PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. On the phone, open the menu, then Link a browser: it shows an address and a 6-digit code.

      tabs

      Saves memory. Click a tab to load it again. Pinned tabs and tabs playing sound stay.
